Types of care in Fort Collins

After your exam, you choose the type of care you want to go through: visit-to-visit care, relief care, or corrective care. The goal is to get you as far along as you want to go.

📆 Relief care is often two to six weeks

📍 Serving Fort Collins area

The short answer

Dr. Irwin works with people on visit-to-visit care, when the sole goal is to get the pain out of the area; on relief care, typically two to six weeks, to increase range of motion and decrease muscle guarding and pain; and on a corrective style of care that rehabilitates the area with strength-based exercises. At the end of the day, the type of care is your choice.

So our new patient exam is the pillar of treatment in the clinic. It is quite a bit more lengthy than most clinics you go to.
