A complete health history

The exam starts by getting to when the problem potentially started, not just when it started this time. Dr. Irwin works through the quality of your pain, where it is, and whether it radiates, and talks through any surgeries you have had and symptoms you have had over the course of your life.

A full evaluation, before any adjustment

In the exam room, the evaluation starts with range of motion and palpation of the body and spine. He is looking at how areas connect: some neck issues come from shoulder issues, and some mid-back issues come from shoulder issues, and vice versa. Some low back injuries can be related to pelvic unleveling or hip bursitis. People sometimes come in saying they have sciatica, and it might be more of a piriformis, a muscular issue, and/or degenerative disc disease in the lower part of the back.

From there he goes through full motion palpation and muscle testing. If needed, he does full-scale orthopedic and neurological evaluations, trying to really pinpoint the area the problem is coming from before he even lays a finger to adjust.

Digital x-rays, when they are relevant

If the exam shows that x-rays of the area are needed, to see if there are further issues to know about before adjusting, he will recommend a set or a full set of digital radiographs. In his words, it is one thing for a mechanic to lay their hands on the hood and say you have a bad alternator. It is another to open the hood, do a full evaluation, and test it while looking at it. X-rays are usually taken, especially when pain is one of the primary drivers of the visit. Films can also show anterolisthesis or spondylolisthesis, which he says pop up all the time on x-ray, and are another reason to take films when those indications are there.

The report of findings

After the evaluation, sometimes the doctor will choose to adjust on the first day. If it is a complex case, he may spend time going through the films and the exam before adjusting, and set up an appointment for you to come back. On that visit he reviews the results of the examination, goes through the x-rays, and then establishes the type of care you want to go through.

That choice is yours. Some people want visit-to-visit care just to get the pain out of the area. Others want relief care over about two to six weeks, or a corrective style of care that includes strength-based rehabilitation. Read about the types of care. His goal is to get you as far along as you want to go, and back to as healthy as you want to be.
