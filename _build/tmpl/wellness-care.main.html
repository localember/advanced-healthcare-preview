What is wellness care?

A number of patients, after they have either done the relief set of care and/or a full corrective form of care in the office, choose to move on to what Dr. Irwin would consider wellness care. This type of care is designed just to maintain the results they received through that initial set of care. Its goal is to help maintain good motion and function in an area.

It follows the path described on the types of care page. You do not start here. You arrive here after that first set of care, if you want to keep the results.

How often do people come in?

Wellness care is anywhere from every other week adjusting all the way up through once a month adjusting in the clinic. So every other week, every three weeks, or one time a month in the office.

What does a plan look like?

Patients are encouraged to continue with the stretching and flexibility they have been taught, as well as their strength-based rehabilitation, so they can maintain good moving forward. A lot of times people will also elect to do massages on one of the massage clubs in the clinic, and some patients even elect to do spinal decompression therapy once a month just to maintain good healthy function of the muscles.
