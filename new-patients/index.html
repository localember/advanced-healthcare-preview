The short answer

Your first visit is a complete health history and a full evaluation, not a one-page form and a quick adjustment. If x-rays are relevant, they are taken in the clinic. Sometimes Dr. Irwin will adjust the same day. So our new patient exam is the pillar of treatment in the clinic. It is quite a bit more lengthy than most clinics you go to.
