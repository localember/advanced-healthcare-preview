What happens at your first chiropractic visit in Fort Collins?

Your first visit at Advanced Healthcare in Fort Collins is a thorough new patient exam, not a one-page form and a quick adjustment. We take a complete health history and evaluate how you move before any adjustment. If x-rays are needed, we take them here in the clinic. Some people are adjusted the same day.

The exam is the foundation of care at Advanced Healthcare. It is more lengthy, and more in depth, than the brief visits many clinics offer. A typical short visit might use a one-page intake, talk through symptoms, and move straight to an adjustment. We take a deeper look first, so we can pinpoint where the problem is coming from.

If the case is more complex, Dr. Irwin may have you return for a report of findings and a conversation about types of care before treatment begins. Either way, the goal of the first visit is to understand the cause, not just to get you in and out quickly.

What does the new patient exam include?

We start with a complete health history: when the problem started, not just this episode, the quality of the pain, whether it radiates, any surgeries, and symptoms over the course of your life. Then we evaluate range of motion and palpation, and, when needed, muscle testing and orthopedic and neurological tests, before any adjustment.

The evaluation looks at how areas of the body relate. Neck symptoms can come from the shoulder. Mid-back issues can too. Low back pain can be tied to pelvic unleveling, hip bursitis, or a muscular issue such as the piriformis, even when someone comes in saying they have sciatica. Degenerative disc disease in the lower back can also be part of the picture.

The point of this work is to pinpoint the area the problem is coming from before Dr. Irwin begins an adjustment. That is what makes the first visit at this Fort Collins chiropractic office different from a quick, symptoms-only stop.

Do you take x-rays on the first visit?

If the exam shows we need to see more before we start adjusting, we recommend digital x-rays, taken here in the clinic. That is especially common when pain is a primary reason for the visit. Films can also reveal findings such as anterolisthesis or spondylolisthesis. Seeing what we are working with helps us treat the cause.

We do not treat imaging as a formality, and we do not skip it when the exam shows it matters. If films are recommended, they are taken in the clinic, so you do not have to go somewhere else to get a basic set of digital radiographs.

Do you adjust on the first day?

Sometimes, yes. After the evaluation, Dr. Irwin may adjust you on the first day when that is appropriate. If the case is more complex, he may go through the exam and any films first, then have you return to review the findings before care begins. That choice is based on what the exam shows, not on a set routine.

When a return visit is needed, that visit is the report of findings. It is time set aside to review the examination, go through any x-rays, and then establish the type of care you want to go through.

What is a report of findings?

A report of findings is a return visit used when the case is more complex. We review the exam, go through any x-rays that were taken, and then establish the type of care you want to go through. It is a conversation about what we found and what your options are, not a plan decided for you.

From there, you choose how to proceed, from visit-to-visit, relief, or corrective care to wellness care later on, if you want it. Our goal is to get you as far along as you want to go, and as healthy as you want to be.

What does care cost, and do you take insurance?

No referral is needed to book a first visit at Advanced Healthcare in Fort Collins. We are in network with BCBS and UHC, and we go over the costs with you before anything begins, so you know what to expect. Call our office at (970) 472-8333 with any questions about your plan.
